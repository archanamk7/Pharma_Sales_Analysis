# Pharma Product Sales Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze pharmaceutical sales performance across products, product classes, years, countries, and distribution channels.

The dashboard transforms raw pharmaceutical sales data into meaningful visualizations and KPIs that help users understand sales performance, product demand, and business trends.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to provide a business intelligence dashboard that helps stakeholders:

- Identify the highest-selling products
- Analyze yearly sales trends
- Compare product-class performance
- Monitor total sales and quantity
- Analyze sales across different countries
- Compare Hospital and Pharmacy channels
- Support data-driven business decisions

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Quantity KPI
- Average Price KPI
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Sales Trend by Year
- Sales by Product Class
- Country-level sales analysis
- Distribution channel analysis
- Interactive filters and slicers

### Available Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:

- Country
- Year
- Distribution Channel

The visualizations and KPI cards update automatically based on the selected filters.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

- Products contributing the highest sales revenue
- Changes in sales performance across different years
- Differences in performance between product classes
- Countries generating higher sales
- Differences between Hospital and Pharmacy channels
- Overall sales volume and quantity trends

1. Download the `pharma-sales-analysis.pbix` file.
2. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Explore the dashboard and interact with the available filters.
4. Select different countries, years, and channels to analyze sales performance.
5. Interact with the charts and KPI cards to explore business insights.
